AI Agent Development

I designed and taught this university-funded general education course around nine labs on building AI agents with a visual workflow editor. Students progress from configuring prompts and tracing variables to calling tools, retrieving documents, storing records, and coordinating specialized agents. In a simulated drone-inspection capstone developed with Shaoxing fire and rescue personnel, students combined tool outputs and source evidence into a brief for human review.
