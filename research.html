Completed classroom study

Epistemic Agency and Knowledge Integration in AI Agent Design

I studied how undergraduates use execution evidence to explain the behavior of AI agents they build.

I designed debugging tasks around retrieved documents, tool outputs, and workflow logs. In a five-week study with 76 students across two classes and three assessments, both classes reported greater epistemic agency and earned higher scores on explanations of how their agents worked. The two gains did not consistently track each other for individual students.
