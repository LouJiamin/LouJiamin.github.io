Completed classroom study

Epistemic Agency and Knowledge Integration in AI Agent Design

I examined how undergraduates tested and revised the AI agents they built, and how their explanations of agent behavior changed over five weeks.

In a switching-replication study, two classes comprising 76 students completed three assessments and received the intervention in successive periods. Students used retrieved documents, tool outputs, and workflow logs to debug their agents. After the intervention, both classes reported greater epistemic agency and gave stronger mechanistic explanations. At the individual level, gains on the two measures were not consistently associated.
