Current research

Automated Adaptive Feedback in Instructional Games

I study how an instructional game could use a student’s errors to select a hint or a follow-up task.

I compared game-based and standard activities in Database Systems (38 and 40 students, respectively) and Web Development (44 and 38). I designed a feedback policy that uses error types and repeated attempts to select hints or follow-up challenges. In a proposed follow-up study, I would compare adaptive task selection with a fixed sequence in Starisle Routes, a six-level database game.
